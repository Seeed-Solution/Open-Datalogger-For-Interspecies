# Vision

AI in the Wild connects field research questions with reusable AI, sensing, and edge computing methods.

## What We Are Building

This repository is a shared foundation for projects that use field devices and AI to study wildlife. It should help teams:

- Turn ecological questions into implementable projects.
- Choose appropriate sensing devices and data formats.
- Reuse AI capabilities across species and sites.
- Share results responsibly.
- Grow from documentation and templates into validated reference implementations.

## What We Are Not Building Yet

- A single universal wildlife recognition model.
- A heavy software framework before real projects define the requirements.
- A public dump of sensitive field data.
- Automated ecological conclusions without expert review.

## Long-Term Direction

The project should become a bridge between researchers, conservation practitioners, hardware builders, and AI engineers. The strongest outputs will likely be:

- Research playbooks.
- Field deployment recipes.
- Dataset and metadata standards.
- Reusable validation tools.
- Edge AI reference pipelines.
- Case studies with clear limitations.
