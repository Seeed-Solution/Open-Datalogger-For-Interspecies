# Camera Nodes

Camera nodes can collect images or video for detection, classification, tracking, and individual identification.

## Planning Checklist

- Target species size and movement speed.
- Day/night conditions.
- Trigger mode.
- Field of view and placement height.
- Storage and power budget.
- Connectivity and retrieval plan.
- Sensitivity of location and media.

## Metadata to Record

- Device ID.
- Camera model and lens.
- Firmware version.
- Resolution and frame rate.
- Trigger settings.
- Time synchronization method.
- Deployment site.
