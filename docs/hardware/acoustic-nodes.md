# Acoustic Nodes

Acoustic nodes collect sound for species presence, vocal behavior, stress signals, and soundscape analysis.

## Planning Checklist

- Target frequency range.
- Sampling rate and bit depth.
- Microphone directionality and weatherproofing.
- Recording schedule.
- Storage and battery life.
- Expected background noise.
- Human voice privacy considerations.

## Metadata to Record

- Device ID.
- Microphone model.
- Sampling configuration.
- Recording schedule.
- Gain settings.
- Enclosure type.
- Deployment site.
