# Environmental Sensors

Environmental sensors help connect animal observations with habitat conditions.

## Possible Measurements

- Temperature.
- Humidity.
- Rainfall.
- Water level.
- Soil moisture.
- Light.
- Vegetation proxies.

## Planning Checklist

- Sensor calibration.
- Sampling interval.
- Weatherproofing.
- Power source.
- Maintenance interval.
- Location precision and sensitivity.
