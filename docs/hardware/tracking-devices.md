# Tracking Devices

Tracking devices can provide movement, home range, migration, and social proximity data.

## Device Types

- GPS.
- VHF.
- LoRa.
- BLE proximity tags.
- PIT tags.

## Planning Checklist

- Animal size and welfare constraints.
- Fix interval and battery life.
- Data retrieval method.
- Attachment method.
- Expected location accuracy.
- Permit and ethical review requirements.
