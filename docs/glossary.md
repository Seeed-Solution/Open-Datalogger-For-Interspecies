# Glossary

## AI Terms

- **Detection**: Locating an object or event in an image, video, audio recording, or sensor stream.
- **Classification**: Assigning a category such as species, call type, or behavior.
- **Embedding**: A numerical representation used to compare similarity.
- **Re-identification**: Matching the same individual across different observations.
- **Tracking**: Following objects or individuals through time.
- **Edge AI**: Running AI models near the data source, often on field devices.

## Wildlife Research Terms

- **Individual**: A specific animal that can be recognized across observations.
- **Observation**: A recorded event involving an animal, environment, or device.
- **Occupancy**: Whether a species is present in a site during a period.
- **Carrying capacity**: The ability of a habitat to support a population over time.
- **Life history**: Longitudinal information about survival, reproduction, health, and movement.
