# Individual Identification & Lifetime Tracking

## Research Goal

Understand whether a new observation belongs to a known individual and build a long-term life-history record.

Key questions:

- Is this the same individual observed before?
- How does its range or migration route change?
- Did it reproduce?
- How do survival, health, and behavior change over time?

## Traditional Methods

- Ear tags, wing tags, bands, PIT tags, VHF, or GPS collars.
- Manual photo comparison.
- Natural marks such as stripes, spots, scars, tail flukes, or facial features.

## Pain Points

- Capture and anesthesia can create risk.
- Collars and tags may be expensive or unsuitable for small species.
- Manual comparison is slow and hard to scale.
- Data is often scattered across teams, seasons, and devices.

## What AI Can Do

- Detect animals in images or video.
- Compare individuals using visual embeddings.
- Link records across devices and time.
- Build individual timelines and life-history profiles.
- Highlight uncertain matches for expert review.

## Possible Data

- Camera images and video.
- GPS, LoRa, VHF, or BLE tracking records.
- Acoustic observations.
- Environmental data.
- Field notes and manual survey records.

## Recommended Techniques

- Object detection.
- Individual re-identification.
- Image embeddings.
- Multi-object tracking.
- Time-series analysis.

## Recommended Devices

- reCamera.
- XIAO ESP32S3 Sense.
- LoRa Tracker.
- Infrared camera traps.
- GPS or VHF devices.
