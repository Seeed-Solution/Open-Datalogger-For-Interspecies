# Population Monitoring

## Research Goal

Estimate species presence, abundance, and population change using field observations and AI-assisted analysis.

Key questions:

- How many animals are present?
- Is the population increasing, decreasing, or stable?
- Where and when is the species detected?
- What is the confidence level of the estimate?

## Traditional Methods

- Transect surveys.
- Camera trap review.
- Mark-recapture studies.
- Nest or den counts.
- Manual occupancy surveys.

## Pain Points

- Detection probability changes with habitat, season, camera placement, and species behavior.
- Counting the same individual multiple times can bias results.
- Manual review of camera and audio data is slow.
- Small datasets can make model evaluation difficult.

## What AI Can Do

- Detect and classify animals in images, video, or audio.
- Count individuals in frames or clips.
- Support occupancy and abundance modeling.
- Reduce manual review by filtering empty media.
- Estimate uncertainty and surface cases for expert review.

## Possible Data

- Camera trap images and videos.
- Acoustic detections.
- Transect records.
- Environmental covariates.
- Human validation labels.

## Recommended Techniques

- Object detection.
- Species classification.
- Counting models.
- Occupancy modeling.
- Active learning for annotation.

## Recommended Devices

- Camera traps.
- reCamera.
- Acoustic nodes.
- Environmental sensors.
