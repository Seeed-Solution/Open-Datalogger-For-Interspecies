# LLM-Assisted Analytics

Large language models can support research workflows, but should not replace expert interpretation.

## Useful Tasks

- Summarizing field notes.
- Searching project documentation.
- Drafting dataset cards and reports.
- Converting unstructured notes into reviewable structured records.
- Helping researchers explore model outputs.

## Risks

- Hallucinated conclusions.
- Hidden uncertainty.
- Leakage of sensitive field data.
- Misinterpretation of species behavior.

## Recommended Practice

- Keep humans in the loop.
- Use source-linked outputs.
- Avoid sending restricted data to external services without approval.
- Treat generated summaries as drafts.
