# Computer Vision

Computer vision can support detection, classification, tracking, behavior analysis, and individual re-identification.

## Common Tasks

- Empty-frame filtering.
- Species detection and classification.
- Individual re-identification.
- Multi-object tracking.
- Behavior recognition.
- Habitat and vegetation change detection.

## Field Constraints

- Night images and infrared lighting.
- Motion blur.
- Occlusion and partial bodies.
- Seasonal appearance changes.
- Device angle and placement differences.
- Low sample counts for rare species.

## Recommended Practice

- Keep raw media, annotations, model outputs, and review status traceable.
- Report confidence and uncertainty.
- Validate across sites, seasons, and device types.
- Use human review for sensitive decisions.
