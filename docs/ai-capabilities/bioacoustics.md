# Bioacoustics

Bioacoustics uses audio data to study species presence, vocal behavior, communication, disturbance, and ecosystem soundscapes.

## Common Tasks

- Sound event detection.
- Species or call-type classification.
- Vocal activity rate analysis.
- Acoustic anomaly detection.
- Noise filtering.
- Clustering unknown calls for expert review.

## Field Constraints

- Wind, rain, insects, water, vehicles, and human voices.
- Microphone placement and enclosure effects.
- Large files and limited bandwidth.
- Regional vocal variation.
- Limited annotated data.

## Recommended Practice

- Store device and microphone metadata.
- Preserve timestamps and timezone information.
- Keep annotation guidelines with datasets.
- Validate models on noisy negative examples.
