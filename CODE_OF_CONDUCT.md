# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative community for people working across wildlife research, conservation, hardware, and AI.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different disciplines, experience levels, languages, and field contexts.
- Assume good intent, while being open to correction.
- Credit research teams, local partners, Indigenous knowledge holders, and data contributors appropriately.
- Discuss sensitive species and location data responsibly.
- Keep disagreements focused on ideas, evidence, and project goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing sensitive wildlife, site, or personal data without permission.
- Misrepresenting research ownership, permits, or dataset rights.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose restricted field information.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers. Maintainers should review reports promptly and take appropriate action.
